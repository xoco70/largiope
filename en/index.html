Our story

Paul Bezert settled in Caudiès-de-Fenouillèdes in 2023, in the north-west of the Agly valley. The Fenouillèdes is the Occitan corner of Catalan Country: Occitan was spoken here while the rest of the Roussillon spoke Catalan. They also say it rains a little more here than elsewhere… that's not true.

Trained under Loïc Roure at Domaine du Possible, Paul farms 6.5 hectares of vines at 400 m elevation, on schistous marls, in a wild, unspoiled setting. The winery is based in Saint-Martin.

Away from the tourist trails, the estate offers a guided walk through the vines followed by a tasting, bookable on Viny'aqui, to discover the winemaker's work through the seasons and the grape varieties.

Our commitments

The vines are farmed organically, leaving plenty of room for wildlife: birds, insects and sheep share the plots. In the cellar, everything is vinified as whole bunches, with no sulphur during winemaking: only 10 mg/l at bottling.

  • In the vines Organic farming, sheep and wildlife between the rows.
  • In the cellar Whole-bunch vinification.
  • Sulphur None during vinification, 10 mg/l at bottling.

Why the argiope?

The wasp spider (Argiope bruennichi) is a spider striped yellow, black and white, found in summer in the tall grass between the vine rows. Despite its wasp-like looks, it is completely harmless to humans.

Its signature: a zigzag ribbon of silk woven at the centre of its web, the stabilimentum. It is thought to help attract insects, or to stop birds from flying into the web. Its name comes from Argiope, a nymph of Greek mythology.

She only settles where grass grows and insects abound: seeing her in the vines is the sign of a living soil. She is the one who gave the estate its name.

Our wines

Walk and tasting

A guided walk through the vines, off the beaten track, to discover the winemaker's work through the seasons, followed by a tasting of the estate's wines. The visit always takes place in two stages:

  1. In the vines A walk through the plots to talk about the terroir, the grape varieties, how the vines are managed and the wildlife that shares the rows.
  2. At the estate A tasting of the estate's wines, to find in the glass what you have just seen in the vines.

Frequently asked questions

How long does the visit last?

About 2 hours: a guided walk through the vines, followed by a commented tasting at the estate.

What does the walk and tasting include?

A guided tour of the vineyard, a commented tasting of 4 to 6 of the estate's wines and a small plate of tapas.

How much does the visit cost?

€20 per adult, free for children. Tasting and plate of tapas included.

Do I need to book?

Yes, the estate can only be visited by appointment, at least 2 days in advance. Book online with the calendar on this page or on Viny'aqui.

How many people per group?

From 2 to 10 people, for a small-group visit.

Which languages is the visit available in?

French, English, Spanish and Catalan.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes, cancellation is free up to 1 day before the visit.

Where is the estate?

In Caudiès-de-Fenouillèdes (66220), in the Pyrénées-Orientales, France, in the heart of the Fenouillèdes, north-west of the Agly valley. The vines grow at 400 m altitude.

What wines does the estate make?

Raw old-vine wines, farmed organically, vinified as whole bunches, with no sulphur during winemaking (10 mg/l at bottling). Reds, a white and a rosé, made from carignan, syrah, lledoner pelut and macabeu.
